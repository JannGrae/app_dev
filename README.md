# My Liberation Notes

**My Liberation Notes** is my favorite K-drama. It's about three siblings who feel tired and stuck in life. They want to be free and happy.

---

## Main Characters

- Yeom Mi-jeong
- Yeom Chang-hee
- Yeom Ki-jeong
- Mr. Gu

> "I want to live a life where I feel at ease just by being myself." – Mi-jeong

## What I Like

1. It’s very calm and deep  
2. The characters feel real  
3. The story is about healing and being true to yourself
